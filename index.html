For flight instructors

FlyCFI is a student tracker for CFIs. A real Part 61 syllabus, ACS-graded lessons, hours against the regulation, endorsements that print onto labels, and an app of their own for each student.

What it does

Syllabus

A syllabus you can teach from

55 private and 40 commercial lessons in training order, each one opening to its objective, lesson plan, FAA procedure step by step, completion standards, key elements, common errors and the ACS risk elements — verbatim, never paraphrased.

Debrief

Grade it on the ramp

Tap a level against the maneuvers the lesson actually plans, add the hours and the landings, write the three lines that matter, and sign it off. The 61.87(d) pre-solo items fill themselves in as you teach.

Records

The 61.189 record, kept for you

Every endorsement given and every test recommendation, with the keep-until date on each row. It prints, and so does the DPE packet.

Endorsements

AC 61-65K, onto a label

The current wording, with the guardrails that say what is not yet met. Print straight onto Avery 5163, 5164 or 5160 — with a skip count for a half-used sheet and an alignment sheet so you do not waste a box.

Experience

61.109(a), requirement by requirement

Summed from the flights you logged, never stored as a running total. Where the app genuinely cannot know — distance flown, which airport — it says so and asks you, instead of showing a zero.

Library

Build the packet once

Your links, in folders. Attach a set to a lesson and it is there for every student who reaches it, and drops into their homework in one tap.

Your students get their own app

Invite a student with an eight-character code. They see what is next and the brief you wrote for it, where they are in the syllabus, your went-well / needs-work / next notes, their homework, their hours and their endorsements — and they can write back to you.

You can cut access off at any moment, and it takes effect at once.

What a student can never see

  • Your private note on a debrief
  • Their citizenship record or any scan of a document
  • Anything at all about another student
  • Your rates, their balance, any payment record
  • The levels you have graded them at

Not a setting. The database hands a student session a short list of views that do not contain those columns.

Built by a working CFI

FlyCFI exists because the syllabus in it is a real one, used with real students, not a demo filled in to make the screens look busy. The regulatory text is the FAA’s own and is carried word for word. Where the app cannot answer something, it says so rather than guessing.

It is a record-keeping tool. It is not a substitute for 14 CFR, the ACS, the handbooks, or your judgement as an instructor — and it is not anybody’s logbook.
