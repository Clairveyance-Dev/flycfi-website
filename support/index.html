Common questions

How does a student get into the app?

Open their record, go to the Student app tab, and press Make a code. They download FlyCFI, make an account, choose “I am a student” and type the code. It works once and expires in fourteen days.

Can another instructor see my students?

No. Records belong to one operation, and that is enforced by the database rather than by the app.

Is this my logbook?

No. It is the instructor’s record of training given — what 14 CFR 61.189 asks you to keep. Your student’s logbook is still theirs, and it is the one that counts.

Why does a requirement say “your instructor confirms this one”?

Because the app records hours, not distance or which airport a landing was at. Five of the 61.109(a) and 61.129(a) requirements are written in those terms. Tick the qualifying flight on the debrief and the app can answer it from then on.

How do I delete my account?

App & account → My account → Delete my account. For an instructor that removes the operation and its records. For a student it removes their sign-in and leaves the training record with their instructor, who is required to keep it for three years.

Reporting something urgent

If you think somebody has reached data they should not have, write to support@flycfi.app with “security” in the subject and we will look at it the same day.
