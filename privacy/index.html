Privacy policy

Last updated November 2026. This is the same text that appears inside the app, under App & account → Privacy policy.

FlyCFI is made by Clairveyance. This policy covers the FlyCFI app and the service behind it.

The short version

  • An instructor’s records are visible to that instructor and to nobody else.
  • A student sees their own training record and nothing about any other student.
  • What a student marks while reading is private to that student — their instructor cannot see it.
  • We do not sell anything to anyone, and there is no advertising in this app.
  • There is no analytics SDK and nothing that tracks you across other apps or websites.
  • Crash reports are collected, and they are stripped of anything that could identify a person before they leave your device.

What is stored

Because this is a record-keeping app for flight training, most of what it holds is entered deliberately by an instructor about their own students.

  • Your account: email address and an encrypted password, held by our authentication provider.
  • Your operation: its name, your name, your certificate number and expiry, your rates and the aircraft and airports you set up.
  • Each student: name, date of birth (used only to enforce the age floor and to flag a minor), contact details you enter, their course, every lesson and grade, flights, endorsements issued, test results, homework and notes.
  • Where an instructor records a 49 CFR 1552 citizenship check, the fact of the check and any document image they attach. This is held apart from the rest of a student record, under tighter rules, and is never visible in the student app.
  • Messages between an instructor and a student, and anything an instructor shares with a student.
  • What you keep while reading the FAA library: the paragraphs you tap to save and the pages you bookmark. See below — these are private to you.
  • Feedback you send us, with your app version and build.

What is NOT stored

  • No payment card details ever touch this app or our database. If card payment is added it will run through a payment processor and we will never see the number.
  • No location. The app does not ask for it and does not use it.
  • No contacts, no calendar, no microphone.
  • Photos only when you choose one to attach, and only the one you pick.

Who can see it

Access is enforced by the database, not by the app. An instructor can read the records of their own operation. A student, once invited, can read the parts of their own record listed in the app under “What they see” — and cannot read any row belonging to another student, nor the instructor’s private notes, nor anything about money.

Our engineers can reach the database to operate and support the service. We look at an individual record only when you ask us to, or where we must to fix a fault or meet a legal obligation.

What you keep in the books

The FAA library is the same for everybody. What you marked in it is not, and it is nobody else’s business — including your instructor’s. A paragraph you keep and a page you bookmark are stored in your own account so they survive a reinstall and follow you to another device, and the database will not return them to any other account. Your instructor cannot see them, and there is no screen anywhere that would show them.

That is deliberate. Reading notes are not a training record, and an instructor who could read them would change what a student dares to mark.

Where the regulations come from

The regulations, the AIM, the Pilot/Controller Glossary and the Advisory Circulars are not kept in FlyCFI. Tapping one opens FlyRegs, a separate Clairveyance app that keeps them current. Nothing about you or your students is passed across when it opens — the link names the document and nothing else.

Source content

The handbooks, the ACS and the other FAA documents in the library are published by the U.S. Federal Aviation Administration and are in the public domain. We extract and index their text so it can be searched and read in the app; we do not alter the official content.

Crash reports

When the app crashes it sends a report to Sentry, our error-reporting provider, so the fault can be found and fixed. A report says where the code broke, on what device, and on which build.

It is deliberately stripped first. Your session is identified by an opaque account id and nothing else — no email address, no name. Email addresses are removed from any message, web addresses are cut back to the path so a database filter cannot carry a student’s name, and request bodies, headers and console output are dropped entirely. No student record is ever sent.

One thing is added at Sentry’s end rather than sent by the app: a report arrives over an internet connection, and a connection reveals roughly where it came from. We have switched off the storing of IP addresses on our Sentry project, so neither the address nor a location derived from it is kept.

This is not analytics. Nothing is recorded about what you look at or how often you use the app.

Where it is stored

On Supabase, in the United States. Supabase is our data processor and does not use your data for its own purposes. Crash reports go to Sentry, also in the United States. Those two are the only companies that hold anything of yours on our behalf.

Children

FlyCFI is for flight instructors and their students. Accounts are not offered to children under 13, and the app refuses to create a student record with a date of birth under 13. A student under 18 is marked as a minor, and an invitation to the student app is expected to go to a parent or guardian.

Your choices

  • You can see everything about yourself in the app.
  • An instructor can correct or remove any student record they entered.
  • A student can ask their instructor to correct anything about them.
  • You can delete your account from App & account → My account.

Deleting your account

Deleting an instructor account closes your sign-in and schedules the operation and its records for deletion. Deleting a student account detaches it from the training record and closes the sign-in; the training record itself belongs to the instructor and stays with them, because an instructor is required by 14 CFR 61.189 to keep it for three years.

If you want something removed sooner than that, or want a copy of what we hold, write to support@flycfi.app.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app will say so rather than quietly updating the date.
